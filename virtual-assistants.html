A Letter From Our Founder

A promise, in writing.

The standard we want every Soro virtual assistant to feel, from the first conversation onward.

To the Dreamer,

Before you decide whether Soro is the right place for your journey, there are a few promises I believe you deserve to hear.

I don’t know your story.

I don’t know what sacrifices you’ve made to get here, what dreams keep you awake at night, or what responsibilities you’re carrying on your shoulders. Maybe you’re hoping to support your family, finish your education, buy your first home, or simply build a life with a little more stability and peace.

Whatever brought you here, I want you to know this:

You are more than a résumé. You are more than a skill set. You are more than the number of hours you can work. You are a person whose future matters.

If we have the privilege of becoming part of your story, these are the promises I hope will define every decision we make.

We will never forget there is a person behind every decision.

Every interview. Every placement. Every conversation. Every policy.

Behind each one is someone trusting us with a piece of their future. You have people who believe in you and depend on you. We will never take that responsibility lightly, and we will never reduce your worth to a number on a spreadsheet or a line in a report.

We will never grow by exploiting people.

This promise matters more to me than any business goal we could ever achieve.

If success ever requires us to compromise our integrity or treat people as disposable, then it isn’t success worth having.

There will always be pressure to move faster, charge more, or squeeze a little more value out of the people doing the work. That is not the company we are building.

We want to help grow your value, not extract it.

If something feels unequivocally unjust or immoral, we’ll walk away from it—because no amount of growth is worth sacrificing the people who helped create it.

We will never stop investing in people.

Your career should not stand still because you joined us.

Our hope is that your confidence grows, your opportunities grow, your skills grow, and ultimately your dreams grow with them.

Whether that means learning something new, taking on greater responsibility, or reaching milestones that once felt impossible, we want to be a company that helps you move forward—not one that simply benefits from where you are today.

We promise to celebrate your victories, not just your productivity.

You are not valuable only because of the work you produce.

We’ll celebrate the promotion you earned. The degree you completed. The home you bought. The family you supported. The dream you finally reached.

Because those victories matter just as much as the work that helped make them possible.

Whether your journey with us lasts one year or ten, my hope is that one day you’ll look back and be able to say this was one of the places that believed in you before you fully believed in yourself.

Thank you for taking the time to read this.

If our paths cross, I hope it’s not simply because you’re looking for work.

I hope it’s because you’re looking for people who genuinely care about where that work can take you.

I can’t promise every day will be easy. I can’t promise every opportunity will be perfect.

But I can promise that we will never forget why Soro exists in the first place.

It exists because people matter.

I hope, if you choose to trust us, you’ll always feel that.

Matthew Johnson Founder, The Soro Group
Who We’re Looking For

What kind of virtual assistants does Soro work with?

The short answer: all kinds. We welcome experienced VAs, specialists, career changers, and people taking their first step into remote work.

General VAs

If you are organized, adaptable, and comfortable wearing a few hats, there is plenty of room for you here.

  • Calendars, inboxes, and follow-ups
  • Research, documents, and data entry
  • CRM and day-to-day business support

Good to knowThis is our broadest lane, and no two general VA roles look exactly alike.

Accountants & bookkeepers

Your eye for accuracy can help a growing business stay organized, informed, and on top of the numbers.

  • Transaction entry and reconciliations
  • Invoices and financial administration
  • Organized records and simple reporting

Experience that helpsAccounting, bookkeeping, payroll, or finance administration.

Healthcare support

Experience anywhere in the medical field can translate beautifully into thoughtful, dependable remote support.

  • Medical billing, coding, and claims support
  • Patient scheduling and care coordination
  • Confidential records and admin workflows

Experience that helpsHealthcare, medical administration, coding, billing, records, or patient services.

Sales & business development

You know how to begin conversations, understand a prospect’s needs, and help promising opportunities move forward.

  • Prospecting and professional outreach
  • Lead qualification and appointment setting
  • CRM updates and thoughtful follow-through

What matters mostClear communication, confidence, resilience, and respectful persistence.

No VA experience yet

Everyone starts somewhere. Transferable skills, a good attitude, and a willingness to learn absolutely count.

  • Customer service or office experience
  • School, volunteer, or personal projects
  • Curiosity and comfort learning new tools

Application Requirements

What you’ll need to complete your application.

There are a few practical steps. Nothing needs to be fancy, but please have the right equipment and enough quiet time to complete each one carefully.

Save your results as you go

You will be asked to share screenshots or links for your test results, along with a Loom introduction. Saving each result as you finish will make the application much easier.

Start Your Application

Computer and workspace

You will need a laptop or desktop computer. Tablets are not accepted.

  • Intel i5, Ryzen 5, or equivalent processor
  • 8GB RAM minimum; 16GB preferred
  • Working webcam and noise-canceling headset
  • A quiet place to work
  • Backup power preferred, such as a UPS or generator

Internet and speed test

Your connection must reach at least 25 Mbps download and 10 Mbps upload, with a backup internet connection available.

Run Speedtest by Ookla and save a screenshot showing your download speed, upload speed, ping, and the visible date and time.

Three personality assessments

Complete all three free assessments and submit a screenshot or summary result for each. They help us understand your communication style, work preferences, and team compatibility.

  • DISC AssessmentCommunication style, pace, and decision-making.
  • Enneagram TestMotivation, stress response, and growth patterns.
  • MBTI-style AssessmentWork style, collaboration preferences, and structure versus flexibility.

English assessment

Complete the free EF SET English Quick Check. A B2 (Upper Intermediate) result or higher is preferred. Share your result as a screenshot or direct link.

A short Loom introduction

You will also be asked to record a brief Loom video introducing yourself. Choose a quiet space, check your webcam and microphone, and speak naturally.

Before Your Interview

Come ready to talk about what matters to you.

This is a conversation, not a performance. We want to understand how you work, what you are proud of, and the kind of opportunity you hope to build toward.

A few things to think through

  • The goals and dreams you want to share with us
  • Two or three examples of work, responsibilities, or problems you handled well
  • How you prefer to communicate, learn, and receive feedback
  • Your availability, preferred working hours, and the kind of role that fits your life
  • Questions you want to ask us about Soro, our clients, or what happens next
