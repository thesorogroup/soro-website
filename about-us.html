About Us

More Than a Staffing Company

Soro was founded on a simple belief: opportunity should create hope, not uncertainty. We connect exceptional Filipino talent with businesses that value trust, fairness, and long-term relationships.

The Past

Where the Vision Began

We became aware of an exploitative employment model that left talented professionals working tirelessly while carrying nearly all of the risk themselves. People were expected to give their time, skill, and loyalty under unstable arrangements, with little certainty they would receive the compensation they had earned.

Behind every résumé is a person. A son or daughter. A parent. Someone supporting a family. Someone chasing a future. Someone who deserves to be treated with dignity.

That realization became the foundation of Soro.

We could not change an entire system overnight. But we could accept part of the burden of changing it by building a company that chose a different path —not by asking what we could take from people, but by asking how we could help them build a better future.

Through our application process, we invited people to share their dreams. Again and again, their answers returned to the same hopes:

  • To provide for their families.
  • To help parents who had sacrificed for them.
  • To afford medicine for loved ones.
  • To create stability.
  • To know tomorrow would be a little better than today.

Those are not lavish dreams.
They are deeply human ones.

But our mission extends beyond employment. We want to understand each person’s goals, ambitions, and dreams so we can build a workplace that supports them as individuals—not simply as employees.

Whether someone’s dream is to earn a degree, become a pilot, support aging parents, start a business, or simply provide a better future for their children, we want Soro to become part of that journey.

Success should never end with a paycheck.
It should create possibilities.

Our Mission

The Future

The Dream

Our dream reaches beyond staffing. We believe a successful business can become a force for lasting good.

As Soro grows, our vision is to use its success to establish a charitable organization dedicated to creating opportunities throughout the Philippines. Through education, career development, support for families, and community-led initiatives, we hope to open doors that reach far beyond the workplace.

The programs it provides will be shaped by the real needs we encounter and the voices of the people and communities we serve.

  • Support education.
  • Invest in career development.
  • Strengthen families.
  • Create meaningful community programs.
  • Open doors that may have once felt impossible.

Every client who chooses to partner with Soro becomes part of something larger than a hiring decision.

We're building businesses.

We're opening doors.

We're restoring dignity where it has too often been overlooked.

We're proving success and compassion were never meant to compete.

They were always meant to grow together.
