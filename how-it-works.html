Choose Your Path

One beginning. Two supported journeys.

Both paths begin with a conversation and follow the same people-first promise, with guidance shaped around what each side needs.

Talent Path

From introduction to meaningful work.

1
Connect

Share your goals

Tell us about your experience, strengths, availability, and the kind of work you want to build toward.

Your goals shape the journey.

2
Be Known

Show how you work

We look beyond the resume to understand your communication style, reliability, ambitions, and best-fit environment.

A person, not just a profile.

3
Meet

Explore a fitting role

When there is strong alignment, we prepare you for a thoughtful introduction and a clear conversation with the client.

The right fit should feel mutual.

4
Grow

Begin with support

We help set expectations before day one and stay available as you settle into the role and build confidence.

Support continues after day one.

Client Path

From hiring need to lasting support.

1
Clarify

Define the real need

We learn your goals, workload, team style, responsibilities, and what success should look like in the role.

Clarity makes better hiring possible.

2
Review

Meet carefully screened talent

We assess skills, communication, reliability, and working style before presenting a focused group of strong matches.

Fewer introductions. Stronger reasons.

3
Choose

Interview with clarity

Meet the strongest candidates, ask the questions that matter, and choose the person who fits your team.

A good match works both ways.

4
Build

Onboard with support

We help establish priorities and communication rhythms, then remain available as the partnership develops.

Lasting support builds lasting teams.

Two paths. One shared standard: the right fit should work for everyone.

Support That Stays

Wherever your path begins, support goes with you.

Whether you are a virtual assistant pursuing meaningful work or a business building the right team, Soro is here to listen, guide, and stay involved.
