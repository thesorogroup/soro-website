Build Your First Handoff

What keeps sliding to tomorrow?

The right starting role depends on where the work gets stuck. Compare common handoffs with what should probably stay on your side of the desk.

A sensible starting role

Administrative Support

Best for recurring coordination work that is easy to overlook and expensive to keep carrying yourself.

Hand off

  • Inbox sorting and routine replies
  • Calendar and meeting coordination
  • Documents, notes, and task updates
  • Follow-up reminders

Keep with you

  • Final approvals and sensitive decisions
  • Client strategy
  • Work only you are authorized to do
Good first task: Have one person sort the shared inbox and send you a short decision list each day.

An Honest Fit Check

A good process can be handed off. Mind reading cannot.

You do not need perfect instructions, but the role does need a clear purpose, a point person, and room for questions.

✓ This is probably a good fit

  • The work repeats every day or week.
  • You can explain what “done well” looks like.
  • Someone can answer questions and give feedback.
  • The task matters, but it does not require you personally.

− Tighten this up first

  • The priority changes with no one making the call.
  • The role depends on decisions only a senior leader can make.
  • No one can provide access, context, or feedback.
  • You need licensed advice rather than administrative support.

A Few Fair Questions

The things owners usually want to know.

Short answers now. A real conversation when you are ready.

Do I need a job description before I contact Soro?

No. A rough list of tasks, pain points, or outcomes is enough for the first conversation. We can help shape that into a clearer role.

Do I choose the person I hire?

Yes. We screen and introduce strong matches, but you meet the candidates and decide who feels right for your business.

What should I delegate first?

Start with work that repeats, can be explained, and has a visible result. One well-defined lane is usually easier than handing over everything at once.

What happens after the placement?

Soro stays involved to support expectations, communication, and the working relationship as it develops.

Is remote support right for every task?

No. Keep strategy, sensitive approvals, licensed advice, and decisions that require your authority with the right person on your team.
